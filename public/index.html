The House

A workshop first,
a brand second.

Veneer Casa comes out of a family that has been manufacturing doors and surfaces in North India for years. We know what warps in a Delhi summer, which cores hold a hinge, and why a beautiful leaf is worth nothing if the joint opens in year two.

So we kept the workshop and built the house around it.

How a door gets made

Four stages, and
nothing skipped.

01

Selection

Leaves are pulled from the same log and laid out so the figure runs continuously across the finished face.

02

Pressing

Veneer is bonded to the core under heat and even pressure, then left to settle so it doesn't move later.

03

Edging

Lipping is applied and trimmed flush, so the edge holds hardware and survives daily use.

04

Finishing

Sanded through the grits, sealed, and inspected under raking light before it leaves the floor.
